K KEYBOARD

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At K Keyboard Team, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to transparency regarding the permissions required for the app to function.

1. Information Collection and Use

Based on the permissions you grant, we collect and use data as follows:

I. Voice Typing (Microphone Access)

We request the RECORD_AUDIO permission to provide the voice-to-text feature. Audio is processed in real-time to convert your speech into text. We do not record, store, or share your audio files on our servers.

II. Custom Themes & Fonts (Storage Access)

Access to READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE is required to allow you to use your own photos as keyboard backgrounds and to download/apply custom fonts. These files are processed locally on your device.

III. Translation Feature (Network Access)

When you use the built-in translation tool, the text you input is sent to a secure third-party provider (Microsoft Translator).

  • Data is transmitted via encrypted HTTPS.
  • The text is used only for the translation process.
  • We do not store your translated text or link it to your identity.

IV. Personal Dictionary & Auto-Correction

The app uses READ/WRITE_USER_DICTIONARY permissions to learn your typing habits and save frequently used words. This data is stored locally on your device to provide faster and more accurate suggestions.

V. Advertising & Performance

We use the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) and network state permissions to show relevant advertisements (AdMob/Facebook Ads). We do not track your personal activities or log your keystrokes.

Our advertising partners — including Google AdMob — may use your device's advertising identifier and other data to deliver and measure ads. To learn how Google collects and uses data when you use apps that use its services, please see How Google uses information from apps that use our services.

Your choices: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

2. Data Security & Deletion

We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption and secure local storage, to protect your data. K Keyboard never logs sensitive information such as passwords, credit card numbers, or private conversations.

Regarding Data Deletion: K Keyboard does not store your personal data or translation history on our own servers. However, translation services are provided by third-party providers (Microsoft Translator). These third parties may process and store data in accordance with their own privacy policies. Since we do not have direct access to or control over data stored by these third parties, we cannot directly fulfill data deletion requests for the information processed by them.

3. Children's Privacy

K Keyboard does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our records.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app may contain links to third-party services (such as ad providers or translation APIs). These services have their own privacy policies, and we recommend reviewing them.

5. Consent for Users in the EEA, UK & Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. When you first open the app, a consent form provided through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is displayed, in line with the GDPR and Google's EU User Consent Policy. Personalized ads are shown only if you give consent, and you can change or withdraw your consent at any time from the app's privacy settings.
